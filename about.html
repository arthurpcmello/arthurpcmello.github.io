PORTRAIT / Arthur Mello

HELLO, I'M Arthur Mello

I make music that
means something.

As an aspiring audio engineer, I am influenced by industry professionals who continually find interesting ways to shape how we listen to music. I draw inspiration from many different genres, blending ambient melodies that create comforting imagery with heavier emotions. Whether through piano, electric guitar, or other instruments, I want each composition to create its own atmosphere.

My musical influences come from a wide range of sounds and approaches to production. I'm still developing my own voice as an artist and audio engineer, and this is a space where I'll continue sharing that process.

More about me, my influences, and the way I approach music will be added here soon.

Thanks for listening. — Arthur Mello
